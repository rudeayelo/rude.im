# Product

## Register

brand

## Users

Visitors who know Rude Ayelo or are evaluating his work, perspective, and web experiments. They need a lightweight first impression and direct paths into demos.

## Product Purpose

This site is a personal hub for experiments in frontend, UX, web platform APIs, and product craft. Success means it stays easy to publish to, feels current without becoming precious, and makes the work itself the primary navigation.

## Brand Personality

Quiet, technical, direct.

## Anti-references

Avoid card-heavy portfolio templates, decorative bento layouts that overpower the content, large marketing-style hero treatments, unnecessary borders, tinted panels, and visual polish that makes the site harder to iterate.

## Design Principles

- Keep the homepage small and durable.
- Let experiments be unconstrained by one framework or implementation style.
- Preserve the existing rude.im mood: simple, mostly monochrome, and personal.
- Treat light and dark mode as part of the interface quality.
- Prefer direct links to working demos over explanatory scaffolding.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Maintain readable contrast in light and dark themes, preserve keyboard focus states, respect reduced-motion preferences, and keep interactions usable without relying on hover alone.
